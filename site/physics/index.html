Physics and Collisions

In the platformer courses your code moved every character itself. In this course you hand the work to Godot’s physics engine. A RigidBody2D or RigidBody3D falls, slides, tips over and knocks other bodies around without any movement code. You will drop a crate, build a tower, and then throw a ball at a stack of blocks in 2D and in 3D.

2D

  1. 1. Drop a crate onto the ground

    Make a RigidBody2D with a collision shape and start it at a random angle.

  2. 2. Build a tower with mass and friction

    Use a function to make many blocks, then change mass and PhysicsMaterial.

  3. 3. Throw a ball at a tower

    Aim with the keyboard, launch with an impulse and count the targets you knock out.

3D

  1. 4. Drop a crate in 3D

    The same idea with RigidBody3D, BoxShape3D and three random angles.

  2. 5. Knock down a crate pyramid in 3D

    Aim up and sideways with a 3D direction vector, then throw.
