Physics course · Lesson 5

Knock down a crate pyramid in 3D

Objective. Make a first-person camera that you can turn but not move, and shoot balls straight ahead at a pyramid of crates.

Nested loops build the pyramid

The pyramid has three rows. Row 0 has 3 crates, row 1 has 2 and row 2 has 1, so the inner loop runs 3 - row times. A third loop makes it two crates deep. The strong metal-banded crates go on the bottom row.

A first-person camera

The Camera3D is the player’s eyes. It stays at EYE, at head height, and uses a perspective view (fov is the field of view in degrees), so distant crates look smaller. The arrow keys change two angles, look_left and look_up, and clampf() stops you looking too far. The angles become the camera’s rotation:

eye.rotation_degrees = Vector3(look_up, look_left - 90, 0)

The x rotation tips the camera up and down. The y rotation turns it left and right. A Godot camera looks along its own −z axis, so the extra −90 degrees turns it round to face the crates along +x.

Shoot where you look

eye.global_transform.basis.z is the camera’s own z axis in world space. Its opposite, -basis.z, points straight out of the middle of the screen, through the crosshair. Each shot makes a new ball just in front of the camera and gives it an impulse along that direction.

var aim := -eye.global_transform.basis.z
ball.apply_central_impulse(aim * SPEED * ball.mass)

Gravity pulls the ball down as it flies, so for a far target you need to aim a little above it.

Predict, change, and test

  1. Knock down all 12 crates. How many shots did you need?
  2. Change SPEED to 7. Do you need to aim higher or lower to hit the top crate?
  3. Change eye.fov to 30, then to 100. What happens to the view?
  4. Change the -45 and 45 in the look_left line to -180 and 180. What can you do now?
  5. Change is_action_just_pressed to is_action_pressed and hold Space.
Check the prediction

A slower ball drops more before it arrives, so you aim higher. A small field of view zooms in like a telescope, and a large one shows more but makes everything look further away. With ±180 you can turn all the way round. is_action_pressed is true on every frame the key is held, so you fire a ball 60 times a second. A real game adds a cool-down timer.

Success criteria. You can look around, aim with the crosshair and knock the pyramid down, and you can explain why the ball flies along -basis.z.

Apply it to your project. Swap the red ball for your own imported model. Give the player a limited number of shots, and add a cool-down so holding Space does not fire every frame.
