Fall and land in 3D

Objective. Apply gravity on the Y axis and use floor collision to land.

Keep vertical and horizontal motion separate

Steering sets X and Z velocity. Gravity changes Y. Positive Y points up in 3D, so subtract gravity. After move_and_slide(), the body can tell whether it landed on a floor. The starter respawns below a fall limit so you can keep testing.

if not player.is_on_floor():
    player.velocity.y -= GRAVITY * delta
player.move_and_slide()

Predict, change, and test

  1. Watch the player fall at the start. Predict what halving GRAVITY will do to the time to land.
  2. Change GRAVITY and compare two drops from the same height.
  3. Change -= to +=, observe the direction, then repair it.
Check the prediction

Lower gravity makes the drop take longer. Adding to vertical velocity accelerates upward because positive Y points up.

Success criteria. The player falls, stops on the floor, and respawns after walking off the map.

Apply it to your project. Place the player above a StaticBody3D floor. Update vertical velocity in the physics callback, then call move_and_slide() and test a fall.
