Finish a 3D obstacle course

Objective. Use an Area3D signal to mark the level complete after a jump across a gap.

Use a 3D trigger at the finish

The starter makes two platforms with a gap between them. Falling below the level still uses the same respawn code. The finish pad uses an Area3D instead of the Area2D from your previous course. Its body_entered signal gives the handler the body that entered.

func reach_goal(body: Node3D) -> void:
    if body == player and not finished:
        finished = true
        finish_label.text = "Course complete! Press R to restart."

Predict, change, and test

  1. Walk off the near edge without jumping. Predict what happens to the player and the completion message.
  2. Jump across, switch cameras with C, then walk through the green pad. Check whether your view affects the finish rule.
  3. Change the completion message in GDScript. Test one fall, one successful jump, and a restart.
Check the prediction

A fall returns the player to the start. Entering the goal completes the level in either view. R creates a fresh run.

Success criteria. The gap can be crossed, only contact with the finish pad marks completion, and the camera can switch during the run.

Apply it to your project. Build a short 3D course with a jump and a finish trigger. Save one test for falling, one for reaching the finish, and one for switching cameras while moving.
