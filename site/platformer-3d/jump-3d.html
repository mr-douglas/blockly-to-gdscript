Jump over a 3D obstacle

Objective. Use the floor check to give the player an upward jump speed once.

Give the jump a positive Y velocity

Pressing Space changes vertical speed if the player is on the floor. Gravity then slows the rise, reverses it, and brings the player back down. The sign of the jump is the opposite of the 2D platformer’s because positive Y points up.

if Input.is_action_just_pressed("action") and player.is_on_floor():
    player.velocity.y = JUMP_SPEED
super._physics_process(delta)

Predict, change, and test

  1. Wait to land. Jump without moving. Press Space again while airborne and observe what happens.
  2. Walk towards the pink beam. Jump while moving forward and try to clear it.
  3. Set JUMP_SPEED to 3. Try the same obstacle. Restore the original value.
Check the prediction

Only a grounded press starts a jump. A smaller upward speed reduces the height. At 3 units/s the player cannot clear the pink beam.

Success criteria. You can jump across the beam, land, and jump again. A press in mid-air does not start a second jump.

Apply it to your project. Map Space to a jump action. Use a positive Y velocity and let gravity bring the player back down. Check both a grounded and an airborne press.
