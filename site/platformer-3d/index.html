Build a 3D Platformer

Build a short obstacle course in Godot. Edit and play the GDScript on every lesson page.

After Build a 2D Platformer, reuse your knowledge of input, collision, gravity, and signals in a 3D scene.

  1. 1. Steer a 3D character

    Rotate around Y and move on the X–Z plane.

  2. 2. Fall and land in 3D

    Accelerate downwards and recover after leaving the map.

  3. 3. Jump over a 3D obstacle

    Jump with Space and tune the jump height.

  4. 4. Switch between first and third person

    Press C to switch between an eye-level view and a chase camera.
