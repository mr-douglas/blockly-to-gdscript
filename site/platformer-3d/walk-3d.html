Steer a 3D character

Objective. Turn a CharacterBody3D and move along its own forward direction.

Use the character’s forward direction

In Godot 3D, y points up. The floor lies on the x–z plane. -player.transform.basis.z gives the player’s forward direction. Turn around y, then use the new forward direction to set velocity. move_and_slide() handles collisions.

var turn := Input.get_axis("right", "left")
player.rotate_y(turn * TURN_RATE * delta)
var forward := -player.transform.basis.z
var throttle := Input.get_axis("down", "up")

Predict, change, and test

  1. Turn right without pressing Up. Predict whether the player will change position.
  2. Turn, move forward, and try to walk into the pink block.
  3. Double TURN_RATE and compare how long it takes to turn.
Check the prediction

Turning changes heading while position stays the same. Pressing Up moves along the new heading. The collision shape stops the player at a solid block.

Success criteria. Left and Right rotate the player. Up and Down move it relative to its heading. The obstacle stops it.
