Import Art and Animations

Import a 2D sprite sheet and a rigged 3D character, then use GDScript to play and switch their animations. Finish by importing sound effects and looping music and controlling them from code.

  1. 1. Import and play a 2D sprite sheet

    Import pixel art, slice frames, and play named loops.

  2. 2. Import and animate a 3D character

    Import a character, find animation clips, and blend between idle and walking.

  3. 3. Import and play sound effects

    Import OGG sounds, play them with AudioStreamPlayer at the right moment, and vary their pitch.

  4. 4. Loop background music

    Make a track loop, switch tracks, pause, and change the volume.
