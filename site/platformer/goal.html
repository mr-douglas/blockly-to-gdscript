Finish the level with a signal

Objective. Use an Area2D signal to mark the level complete when the player reaches its goal.

Detect overlap at the finish

An Area2D with a CollisionShape2D can detect a body that enters its space. It does not act as a solid wall. Connect body_entered to a function. That function receives the body that entered and can check whether it is the player.

goal.body_entered.connect(_on_goal_entered)

func _on_goal_entered(body: Node2D) -> void:
    if body != player or completed:
        return
    completed = true
    status.text = "Level complete! Press R to play again."

Predict, change, and test

  1. Jump the gap and walk through the trophy. Predict whether it will stop the player or change the message.
  2. Comment out the connect() line and reach the trophy again. Reconnect the signal and retest.
  3. Move the goal left and change its completion message. Test a fall, a successful run, and a restart. Check that only a successful run shows your message.
Check the prediction

The area lets the player through and sends a notification. The connected function updates the message once. Without the connection, entering the area never calls your function.

Success criteria. Falling respawns the player. Reaching the goal after a successful jump shows the completion message. Pressing R starts a fresh run.

Apply it to your project. Add an Area2D with a rectangle collision shape to your own level. Connect its body_entered signal. Keep the player check and test the whole level from spawn to finish.
