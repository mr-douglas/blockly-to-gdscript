Recover after falling

Objective. Detect a fall and reset both the player’s position and its velocity.

Reset the whole movement state

The level now has a gap. A y value greater than FALL_LIMIT means the player has fallen below the level. Call a named function to reset both position and velocity. Position says where the player is. Velocity says how it will move on the next tick.

func respawn() -> void:
    player.position = START
    player.velocity = Vector2.ZERO

Try it on this page

Open the editor on its own page

Predict, change, and test

  1. Walk into the gap without jumping. Predict where the player will reappear.
  2. Temporarily remove player.velocity = Vector2.ZERO. To make the difference easy to see, change the y coordinate of START in respawn() by setting player.position = START + Vector2(0, -150). Compare the fall after respawning with and without the velocity reset.
  3. Restore both lines. Jump over the gap and check that a successful jump does not reset the player.
Check the prediction

The player reappears at the start. With an elevated spawn and no velocity reset, it carries its downward speed from the fall. Resetting velocity makes it begin falling again from rest.

Success criteria. Walking off the edge restarts the player; a clean jump reaches the other side. You can explain why both position and velocity matter.

Apply it to your project. Add a gap to your own level. Choose a spawn point above a solid floor and a fall limit below your scene. Test a missed jump and a successful one.
