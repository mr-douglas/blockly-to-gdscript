Collect coins and count them

Objective. Use Area2D signals to award points and remove a pickup once.

Make every pickup count once

The level loops over three positions and creates a coin at each one. Each coin’s body_entered signal passes the body that touched it. bind(coin) also passes the specific coin, so the handler can remove that one. queue_free() removes it at the end of the frame.

func collect_coin(body: Node2D, coin: Area2D) -> void:
    if body != player or coin.is_queued_for_deletion():
        return
    coins += COIN_VALUE
    coin_label.text = "Coins: %d" % coins
    coin.queue_free()

Predict, change, and test

  1. Walk through the first coin and then back through its old position. Predict how many points you will have.
  2. Set COIN_VALUE to 5. Predict the label after two pickups, then run your version to check.
  3. Add a fourth coin using a new Vector2 position. Put it where you need to jump to reach it.
Check the prediction

Each coin adds points once because it disappears. Two pickups worth five each give a total of ten.

Success criteria. Coins disappear when touched, the label matches your total, and the added coin can be picked up.
