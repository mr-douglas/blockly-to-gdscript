Fall and land with gravity

Objective. Use gravity to change vertical speed and let collision stop the fall.

Apply gravity every physics tick while in the air

In Godot 2D, positive y points down. Gravity adds to velocity.y, so the player falls faster over time. delta converts acceleration (pixels/s²) to a change in speed (pixels/s). is_on_floor() uses the last move_and_slide() result. The starter puts the player above the floor so you can watch it fall.

func apply_gravity(delta: float) -> void:
    if not player.is_on_floor():
        player.velocity.y += GRAVITY * delta

Open the editable gravity starter

Predict, change, and test

  1. Run the starter. Watch the player accelerate and then stop at the solid floor.
  2. Halve GRAVITY and predict what will happen to the fall time. Run both versions to compare.
  3. Change += to -=. Explain the resulting direction, then fix it.
Check the prediction

With less gravity, the player gains speed more slowly and takes longer to land. Subtracting a positive amount makes the player accelerate up.

Success criteria. The player falls on start, stops on the floor, and can still move sideways. You can explain the difference between velocity and acceleration.

Apply it to your project. Add a gravity constant to your player script. Update the player’s own velocity.y while airborne, then call move_and_slide(). Move the player above your floor in the editor and run the scene.
