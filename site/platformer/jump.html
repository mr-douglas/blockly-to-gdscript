Jump only from the floor

Objective. Use one press and a grounded check to start a jump.

Set an upward speed once

Space is mapped to the action input. Check is_action_just_pressed() so holding the key does not repeatedly jump. Check is_on_floor() so a press in mid-air is ignored. Up is negative y. super._physics_process(delta) runs the previous lesson’s movement and gravity code.

if Input.is_action_just_pressed("action") and player.is_on_floor():
    player.velocity.y = -JUMP_SPEED

Open the editable jump starter

Predict, change, and test

  1. Wait until you land. Press Space and then press it again while airborne. Predict what each press will do.
  2. Change JUMP_SPEED to 240. Try to jump onto the raised platform. Restore 440 and try again.
  3. Temporarily remove the floor check. Test a second press while airborne, then repair the condition.
Check the prediction

Only the first press starts a jump with the floor check. At 240 pixels/s, the jump is too low for the raised platform. Removing the floor check lets another press reset the upward speed in mid-air.

Success criteria. You can jump onto the platform. Holding Space does not keep bouncing, and pressing it again in mid-air does not restart the jump.

Apply it to your project. Add the action input and bind it to Space. Put the jump check before moving the CharacterBody2D, and apply gravity while it is airborne. Test a short jump and a jump onto a higher platform.
