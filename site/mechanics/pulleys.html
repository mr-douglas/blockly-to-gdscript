Two masses over a pulley

Objective. Use Newton’s second law to find the acceleration and tension of two connected particles.

Model two particles released from rest, joined by a light, inextensible string over a smooth pulley. Take g = 10 m/s². The string stays taut. Choose downward for B and upward for A as positive.

Predict, then test

  1. Set A to 2 kg and B to 3 kg. Draw a force diagram for each mass. Calculate the signed acceleration of B and the tension.
  2. Run the model, then scrub to 1 s. Use your acceleration to predict B’s speed and displacement there.
  3. Set both masses to 2 kg. Predict the tension before testing. Then swap the original unequal masses and explain the sign change.

Check your working

Show the equations and answers

For A, T − mAg = mAa. For B, mBg − T = mBa. Add the equations to remove T, then substitute back for tension.

With 2 kg and 3 kg, B accelerates down at 2 m/s² and the tension is 24 N. After 1 s, B is moving down at 2 m/s and is 1 m below its start. Equal 2 kg masses stay at rest and the tension is 20 N. Swapping 2 and 3 makes B’s acceleration −2 m/s², with tension still 24 N.

Try the equation in GDScript

Change one mass and predict both values before running. Compare your result with the diagram.

var mass_a = 2.0
var mass_b = 3.0
var acceleration = 10.0 * (mass_b - mass_a) / (mass_a + mass_b)
var tension = mass_a * (10.0 + acceleration)
print(acceleration)
print(tension)
