A particle on a smooth slope

Objective. Resolve weight parallel and perpendicular to the plane, then predict motion along it.

A particle starts from rest. The plane is smooth, so there is no friction. Take g = 10 m/s² and choose down the plane as positive. In the diagram, the orange arrow shows weight and the blue arrow shows the normal reaction. Arrows show directions.

Predict, then test

  1. Set a 2 kg particle on a 30° slope. Find the component of weight down the plane, the normal reaction, and the acceleration.
  2. Calculate its speed and distance along the plane after 1 s. Run to check.
  3. Double the mass and keep the angle fixed. Predict which readings change. Then set the angle to 0° and explain the result.

Check your working

Show the equations and answers

Down the slope, mg sin θ = ma, so a = g sin θ. Perpendicular to the slope there is no acceleration, so R = mg cos θ.

For 2 kg at 30°, the down-slope force is 10 N, the reaction is about 17.32 N, and the acceleration is 5 m/s². After 1 s, the speed is 5 m/s and the distance is 2.5 m. Doubling the mass doubles both forces and leaves the acceleration unchanged. At 0°, the component along the plane is zero, so the particle stays at rest.
