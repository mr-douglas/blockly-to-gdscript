Two carts that stick on impact

Objective. Use conservation of linear momentum to find a common velocity after a direct impact.

The carts move along a smooth horizontal track and stick together when they collide. Choose right as positive. Weight and the normal reaction balance vertically, so g = 10 m/s² does not enter the horizontal equation. The diagram uses particle positions, with the cart shapes added for visibility.

Predict, then test

  1. Start with A at 2 kg moving at +4 m/s and B at 3 kg moving at −2 m/s. Find the total momentum and the common velocity. Predict which way the joined carts move.
  2. Scrub across the collision. Compare total momentum before and after. Extension: calculate the total kinetic energy at both times.
  3. Keep the velocities. Set A to 1 kg and B to 2 kg, then change B to 3 kg. Predict the direction after impact in each case.

Check your working

Show the equation and answers

mAuA + mBuB = (mA + mB)v. The velocities are signed.

The first run has total momentum +2 kg·m/s. The common velocity is +0.4 m/s, so the carts move right. Total kinetic energy changes from 22 J to 0.4 J. With masses 1 kg and 2 kg the total momentum is zero, so the joined carts stop. With masses 1 kg and 3 kg the common velocity is −0.5 m/s, so they move left.

Try the equation in GDScript

Change just the mass of A to 1 kg. Predict the sign of the output and explain what it means before running.

var mass_a = 2.0
var mass_b = 3.0
