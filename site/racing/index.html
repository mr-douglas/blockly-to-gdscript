Build a Two-Player Racer

Build a local two-player 3D racing game. Start with a fixed overhead view, then build a shared camera and add race rules.

  1. 1. Drive two cars on one track

    Share a fixed overhead view of the whole track.

  2. 2. Pan and zoom to keep both cars visible

    Use a midpoint and fit both dimensions of the screen.

  3. 3. Count laps and decide who wins

    Use ordered checkpoints and a finish line for a complete race.
